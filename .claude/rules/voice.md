# Voice and writing rules

These constraints apply to every piece of shipped content on the site.

## Absolute constraints

1. **No em dashes (—) anywhere in shipped content.** Use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses instead. HTML comments may contain em dashes; rendered text may not.
2. **No consulting platitudes.** Avoid "judgment", "discernment", "thought leadership", "synergy", "the AI whisperer", or similar abstract-wisdom framing.
3. **No abstract-wisdom positioning.** Alex does not claim to possess what trainees lack. He starts with concrete questions.

## Positioning

Alex is **strategy-first, concrete, uncertainty-forward**. His work is:
- Overcoming blockades
- Fixing pain points
- Gaining efficiency
- Gaining quality
- Thinking critically
- Enhancing thinking

Always starting with "what do you actually need?" before touching a tool.

**Dual positioning**: AI trainer + AI visuals producer. Commercial offering includes independent visual work for brands (currently shipping for Promptgorillas, Chris le More). Framing: "for brands that want a distinct visual world, not another stock image."

## Podcast voice

The podcast is "the fun AI podcast" (with his girlfriend Morgan, in Dutch). Tone: messy, funny, sometimes embarrassing. No explainers, no hype. The voice is playful but has a point of view.

## Creative building voice

"I hate AI-slop. My mission is to create AI visuals that always feel intentional and impressive." Strong, direct, personal.

## Homepage voice

"AI is everywhere and most teams are guessing. I help professionals stop guessing. Through training, strategic consulting, and hands-on visual production."

## Placeholder convention

When text needs user input, use bracketed placeholders:
- `[CONTENT: description of what to write, with a starting draft in quotes]`
- `[MEDIA: description of what asset to provide, with format and source notes]`

Always include a starting draft so the user can edit from it rather than starting from scratch.

## Known deferred content

Podcast episode taglines and episode bodies contain existing content (Dutch and English mixed) with multiple em dashes. This is historical content predating V102's voice cleanup. Do not rewrite unless explicitly asked. Flag as known-deferred.

## The single-sentence test

Apply this when in doubt on any copy: **"If I deleted this sentence, would the page still communicate Alex's point of view?"** If yes, the sentence was probably padding. Keep shipped copy tight.
