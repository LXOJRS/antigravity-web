R&D, Consultancy & Workshops

This work begins when the path isn’t obvious yet.

Context

Many teams see the potential of AI but struggle to turn that into action. I help define the groundwork so exploration doesn’t turn into confusion.

02

Focus

I collaborate with professionals from diverse fields to uncover what AI could do for them. Together, we move from first experiments to long-term thinking, always weighing quick wins against reliable, safe implementation.

03

Format

04

Background

My approach is shaped by my experience in film, rhetoric, and business strategy. I look at how language drives choices, and how clear narratives shape outcomes.

05

I step in when things are still forming, when teams are asking what’s possible and where to go next.
