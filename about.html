Origin

I did not come to AI through computer science. I came through a Bachelor in film, literature, and rhetoric, where meaning-making is the central theme. I ended up transferring that skill to a Master’s in Media and Business, where I researched the impact and future of AI on organizational strategy.

04½

Recent build

I built a custom MCP server that pipes the podcast's YouTube analytics directly into Claude. Now I can ask "which episodes pull viewers past the four-minute mark?" and get a real answer in seconds. The point isn't the MCP. The point is that exploration produces tools. That loop is the work.

05

Method

The Value Fit model is a framework I built during my cum laude thesis to answer one question: how does AI affect your strategy within your context and values? Specifically researched for the visual production sector, the model reveals a cyclical motion. AI strategy is rarely something that is built once, it is often a continuous reassessment.

I studied film and literature before I touched AI. Both teach you the same thing: how a piece is built, why a choice lands, what an argument actually is. Prompting is composition. Editing a model's output is the same close-reading I was doing on novels in 2014.
