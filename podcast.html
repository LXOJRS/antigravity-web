AI-Rated Podcast

AI-Rated is where curiosity gets some room.

Concept

In this podcast, my girlfriend Morgan and I talk about AI the way it actually shows up in everyday life. As something that quietly reshapes culture, habits, expectations, and the way people talk about their work. We aim to keep things fun.

Tone

The tone is loose, but the questions are taken seriously. Some episodes circle around online phenomena, synthetic media, or the latest AI oddity that suddenly feels normal.

LISTEN

Goal

The goal is to stay interesting, and frankly, to stay lighthearted. AI-Rated is meant to be accessible, mostly entertaining, occasionally uncomfortable, and often reflective.

Focus

We don't try to keep up with AI news. We pay attention to how people respond to it. That makes the podcast less about answers, and more about noticing patterns and small shifts that usually pass unnoticed.

New episodes are released monthly, and can be found on YouTube and Spotify.
