AI-Generated Visuals

I treat AI-generated visuals as a medium in their own right.

01 / MEDIUM

Medium

What interests me is how AI enables images and motion that wouldn't exist otherwise. Visuals that don't replicate photography, film, or design, but follow their own internal logic. That's where AI shifts from instrument to language.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

Perspective

My goal is to use that language deliberately.

I create visuals that say something because they are AI-generated. Much of this work leans toward an editorial aesthetic: images that feel intentional rather than manufactured. That quality makes them particularly suited for brands that want distinct visual worlds instead of interchangeable content: not just a single striking image, but a recognizable visual system.

CREATE
03 / APPLICATION

Application

The work often begins exploratively, guided by a clear idea or question. I test how far it can stretch within this medium. From there, I design workflows where variables are exposed and controlled: one character, multiple outputs, repeatable lighting.

Because once visuals move beyond a single frame into campaigns or series, consistency decides whether they can scale. Good-looking images are one thing. Reproducible ones demand structure.

04 / CONCLUSION

My background in film shapes how I approach this — I think in terms of visual storytelling. Even when the visuals feel abstract, they're designed to communicate intent, tone, and meaning.

AI visuals are becoming part of everyday visual culture. This work explores how to use the medium with precision, and discipline, before it settles into habit.
